Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Scope and publisher

Creative Muse is published by Matthew Lilley. This policy covers the Creative Muse plugin, these website pages and messages sent directly to its publisher. OpenAI’s handling of ChatGPT data is separate.

Using Creative Muse in ChatGPT

Creative Muse is a skill: instructions that help ChatGPT generate journal prompts and artist’s-date ideas. Its package contains no external tools, connected apps, publisher-operated backend, sign-in system or tracking code. It provides no mechanism for sending your conversation to the publisher.

Generating prompts does not require a connection to the publisher’s website.

OpenAI processes the content you share with ChatGPT. Its handling of that information depends on the applicable OpenAI policies, your account and your settings. Consult OpenAI’s privacy policy and the controls in your ChatGPT account. This policy does not set OpenAI’s retention or deletion rules.

If you choose to send a conversation excerpt to the publisher for support, that excerpt becomes part of your support message.

Visiting these pages

These pages do not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. They use locally hosted styles and images, with no embedded content, third-party fonts or forms. Linked sites have their own privacy policies.

The Nginx web server keeps access logs containing IP addresses, request times, requested URLs, response status codes, referrer information and browser details. Error logs can also contain connection and request information. These logs concern visits to this website and do not contain your conversations with Creative Muse in ChatGPT.

Server logs are configured to rotate daily when non-empty, retaining 14 rotated files for each log. Empty logs are not rotated, so older records can remain for longer than 14 calendar days.

Contacting the publisher

You can contact Matthew Lilley at info@mattlilley.com. If you send an email, he receives your email address, any name you provide, and the message and attachments you choose to send, to handle your enquiry. Messages arrive in a Gmail mailbox, so Google handles the email service as well as any providers involved in sending your message.

Support emails are received in the publisher’s personal Gmail inbox and may be retained indefinitely.

Your rights and contact

For questions about information you share directly with the publisher, use the support page. For ChatGPT conversation or account information, use OpenAI’s privacy resources and account controls.
