Support for Creative Muse

For a question, a problem with a prompt, or feedback about Creative Muse, contact its publisher, Matthew Lilley.

info@mattlilley.com

What to include

A short description of what you asked for and what went wrong is helpful. If you include an example response or a screenshot, remove anything personal you don’t want to share. You don’t need to send your journal entries or your full conversation.

Privacy questions

Use the same contact address for questions about information you send directly to the publisher. See the privacy policy for details about using Creative Muse in ChatGPT and contacting the publisher.
