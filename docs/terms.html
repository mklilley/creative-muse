Terms of service

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The service and its publisher

Creative Muse is published by Matthew Lilley. It offers original morning-pages journal prompts and artist’s-date ideas inside ChatGPT, inspired by The Artist’s Way by Julia Cameron. It is intended to complement the book rather than reproduce or replace it.

Using it through ChatGPT

Creative Muse runs as a skill within ChatGPT. Your access to ChatGPT is governed separately by the OpenAI terms applicable to your account. Creative Muse suggestions are generated responses; consider whether an idea fits your interests and circumstances before acting on it.

Cost

Creative Muse is free to use. Matthew Lilley charges no fee and offers no payments or purchases through the plugin. OpenAI’s own access terms still apply.

Source code and generated responses

The source repository includes an MIT licence, which accompanies the distributed plugin package. These terms do not add a separate claim to ownership of your ChatGPT inputs or generated responses.

Availability

The publisher intends to make Creative Muse available without additional country restrictions. Access still depends on the availability of ChatGPT and the plugin through OpenAI.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy policy for details about ChatGPT data, website visits and support messages. Questions can be directed to the publisher through the support page.
